This knowledge-workspace solution is built for organisations that need operational knowledge to stay usable across onboarding, training, and daily execution. It turns scattered notes, SOPs, shared-drive material, and tribal know-how into one governed system teams can search, trust, and reuse.

## What this solution covers

- Structured knowledge articles, SOPs, and operational playbooks
- Source sync for shared folders, uploads, and evolving reference material
- Searchable guidance for teams, departments, and locations
- Role-based access so the right people see the right information
- Rich media support for screenshots, video, and attachments
- Change history, approvals, and version-aware knowledge updates
- Internal knowledge capture workflows and training-draft support for growing teams

## Best fit

This solution is a strong fit for organisations that are scaling quickly, standardising delivery, or losing time because critical know-how lives in people’s heads, chats, shared folders, and disconnected files.

## Product direction

### 1. Central knowledge hub

- Bring SOPs, checklists, templates, synced source material, and recurring guidance into one structured place.

### 2. Team-friendly search and discovery

- Make internal knowledge easy to find by role, team, location, process, keyword, or connected source.

### 3. Governance and quality control

- Add ownership, review cycles, approval flows, and source scoping so guidance stays current and trusted.

### 4. Scale with the organisation

- Support multiple teams, permission models, content structures, onboarding flows, and future workflow automation.

## Technology Stack

- Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript
- Backend: Node.js, PostgreSQL, search indexing
- Product features: permissions, audit history, source sync, search, and admin controls
