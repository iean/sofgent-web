SofGent builds cross-platform mobile apps with React Native — one codebase, native-feel performance on iOS and Android — with store submission handled end to end.

## What this service covers

- Cross-platform builds for iOS and Android
- Native integrations: push, camera, location, biometrics
- Offline-first data and sync where it matters
- App Store and Play Store submission and review
- Crash reporting, analytics, and over-the-air updates

## Best fit

Teams that need a mobile presence without maintaining two separate native codebases, or extending an existing product to mobile.

## Technology Stack

- Mobile: React Native, Expo, TypeScript
- Backend: Node, PostgreSQL, REST APIs
- Delivery: EAS, App Store Connect, Play Console
