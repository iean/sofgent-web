System maintenance keeps your software stable after launch. That includes bug fixes, security updates, dependency upgrades, performance monitoring, and operational support so the product keeps working as the business grows.

Many teams have software that is already live but no longer has an active engineering owner. In that situation, issues stack up quickly: packages age, bugs remain open, deployments get risky, and small failures turn into expensive downtime. We step in as the delivery team that keeps the system healthy and moving forward.

## What this service covers

- Bug fixing and production support
- Dependency, framework, and infrastructure upgrades
- Security patching and vulnerability remediation
- Performance tuning and query optimization
- Monitoring, alerting, and incident follow-up
- Small feature enhancements and technical debt cleanup

## Best fit

This is a strong fit for businesses that already have working software but need dependable engineering support to keep it fast, secure, and maintainable.

Typical scenarios include:

- A system built by a previous vendor that now needs a new owner
- Internal tools with recurring bugs and no engineering bandwidth
- Customer-facing products that require ongoing release support
- SaaS platforms needing upgrades without disrupting current users

## Maintenance models

### Ongoing support retainer

- A dedicated maintenance lane for recurring fixes, improvements, and release support.

### Stabilization sprint

- A short engagement focused on backlog cleanup, dependency upgrades, and system hardening.

### Incident-response support

- Targeted engineering help when a live system is unstable and needs immediate technical intervention.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you maintain software your team did not originally build?**
Yes. We commonly take over existing systems, review the codebase, and create a practical support path from the current state.

**Do you also handle small improvements, not just bug fixes?**
Yes. Maintenance can include minor feature work, UI adjustments, refactors, and platform upgrades when they fit the agreed scope.

**How do you reduce the risk of breaking live systems?**
We use staging workflows, testing, change reviews, and controlled rollouts to keep maintenance safe.
