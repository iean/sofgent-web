Service 01 · Document Automation

Turn documents into structured data. Automatically.

OCR alone isn't enough. We build end-to-end document intelligence — classify, extract, validate, and route — eliminating manual review entirely.

Intelligent classification
AI identifies document type, language, and structure — even with handwritten or scanned inputs.
Field-level extraction
Pull dates, names, amounts, clauses, and custom fields with >98% accuracy out of the box.
Validation + routing
Confidence scoring, exception handling, and automatic routing to the right workflow or system.
Deployed in 2–3 weeks
From intake brief to production pipeline — not months, not a PoC. A live system your team uses daily.
Typical outcome
6-month manual process → fully automated in 18 days
