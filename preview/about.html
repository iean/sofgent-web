Our story

Most AI never makes it out of the demo.

We kept seeing the same pattern: ambitious AI projects that looked great in a slide deck and then stalled for months in "pilot." Teams burned budget on proofs-of-concept that never touched a real user.

SofGent was built to break that cycle. We combine senior engineering, AI-native tooling, and a tight, fixed-scope process to ship real, production-grade products in weeks. No status theatre. No surprise invoices. Just working software your team actually uses.
