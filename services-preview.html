How fast can you actually ship?
Most projects are scoped and shipped within 2–4 weeks. The exact timeline depends on complexity — a focused MVP takes 2–3 weeks; a full AI system with integrations typically 4–6 weeks. We give you a written timeline before we start.
Do you work with non-technical founders?
Yes — most of our clients aren't engineers. We handle all technical decisions, explain the trade-offs in plain English, and give you working software at the end of each week so you always know where things stand.
What's the difference between the MVP offer and the AI Systems offer?
The MVP offer is for building a new user-facing SaaS product from scratch. The AI Systems offer is for building internal tools, automation pipelines, or AI-powered features on top of existing data or workflows — it doesn't always result in a public-facing product.
Do you keep working with us after launch?
Every service includes 30 days of post-launch support. After that, we offer retainer agreements for ongoing development, maintenance, and new features. Many clients continue working with us month-to-month after the initial project.
What tech stack do you use?
Our default stack is Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, with Supabase or PostgreSQL for data. For AI we use Claude (Anthropic) and OpenAI, with custom RAG pipelines built on top. We adapt to your existing stack if needed.
How much does it cost?
Pricing depends on scope. We don't publish rates because a 2-week focused MVP and a 6-week AI system are very different. Book a free 30-minute call and we'll give you a written estimate within 48 hours — no obligation.
