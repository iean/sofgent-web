# Trust and Claim Substantiation

**Spec ID:** `SPEC-WLR-003`  
**Date:** 2026-07-17  
**Status:** `Approved`  
**Owner:** SofGent web team

## Problem Statement

The site makes strong trust claims that currently outrun the visible supporting evidence. This creates avoidable skepticism, especially for technical or enterprise buyers.

## Evidence

Examples observed on 2026-07-17:

- `0 critical launch bugs across every project we've shipped to production`
- `Every product we've shipped went live without a single critical bug`
- `Three things we do better than anyone`

## Scope

- inventory major trust and guarantee claims
- classify each as keep, soften, prove, or remove
- revise homepage and related pages to align claims with available proof

## Non-Goals

- collecting new customer proof not yet approved for use
- legal review of all marketing statements

## Requirements

- no major claim should remain unsupported by visible proof
- language should remain strong but credible
- trust sections should emphasize evidence over bravado

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] unsupported superlatives and guarantees are revised
- [ ] retained claims are backed by visible proof or concrete framing
- [ ] trust copy is reviewed on homepage and major offer pages

## Verification

- content review against claim inventory
- visual review of affected pages
